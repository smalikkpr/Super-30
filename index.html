Founder's Message

Why we started Super-30

Every nation grows through education. Sadly, our education system is now mostly about marks, exams and rote learning. After years of study, many students are still weak in confidence, leadership, self-expression and practical life skills.

Super-30 was born from this concern. We are not here only to teach subjects. We are here to prepare young people who serve their families, society and country with knowledge, character, confidence and skill.

We believe every child has extraordinary talent. It only needs the right environment, guidance and training to wake up.

Super-30 is not just an institute. It is an idea.

About Us

Our vision and mission

Super-30 selects a small group of students and teaches them in a model environment. It is very different from traditional classrooms.

Our Vision

To build an education system that prepares the youth of Pakistan to world standards in knowledge, ethics, thinking and practical skills. In future, we aim to bring positive change across the country.

Our Mission

To teach and train selected students in an environment where:

  • each student gets personal attention
  • modern teaching methods are used
  • confidence and self-expression grow
  • learning goes hand in hand with technology
  • leadership and character are built
  • students get ready for practical life

Our Motto

Exposure: The more a student sees of the world, the wider the mind becomes.

Knowledge: Right knowledge is the base of success.

Confidence: Confidence turns knowledge into action.

How we teach

The year starts with a special Foundation Month. After that, every class is active, and every student takes part.

Foundation Month

English

  • Written English
  • Spoken English

  • Pronunciation

Computer

  • Web designing
  • Graphic designing

Personality

Classroom culture

Questions and answersDebatesPresentations QuizzesGroup workPractical activities Project-based learning

Teachers prepare weekly lesson plans and teach with slides on a projector or smart board.

Technology skills

Basic: Typing, MS Word, Excel, PowerPoint

Intermediate: Graphic Designing, Video Editing

Advanced: Web Development, Programming, AI Tools, Freelancing (as projects)

Language policy

Urdu for national identity. English for global communication and careers. Every student will be able to introduce themselves, give a presentation, write an email and face an interview in English.

Reading culture

Students read textbooks, other books, science magazines and biographies. Each teacher gives a reading list for the year.
